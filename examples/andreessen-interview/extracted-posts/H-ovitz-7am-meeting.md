## Why this works (annotated)

- **Hook (14 words):** named credentialed authority + named subject (Ovitz, Hollywood) + specific shocking detail (7am meeting + 20 Jaguars). Verb is `says`. No recency words.
- **Second beat:** jumps to specific historical context (1975, 90-year-old industry) — not credential paragraph, not vague setup.
- **Body structure:** a clean three-act story. Act 1 = the 9am-vs-7am hack. Act 2 = the Paul Newman exchange (verbatim three-line dialogue). Act 3 = the phalanx (visual: Armani, Sulka, Jaguars, CAA-1 to CAA-20).
- **Quotes (3):** all verbatim from the transcript. The Paul Newman exchange uses 3 short quotes that read as theatrical dialogue.
- **Sentence length:** every prose sentence under 15 words. Many under 10.
- **Engagement Q:** "Genius — or just ruthless?" — 5 words, binary, opinion-prompting. Reader can answer with one word.
- **Closer:** brand CTA + P.S. product CTA verbatim. Attribution with spaces in parens (`( @pmarca )`, `( @FoundersPodcast )`) so X auto-mention engine renders the tags.
- **Word count:** 158 words (slightly under target — could be padded with more on the Sulka backstory or Ovitz's pre-CAA history per `word-count.md`).

## Clip spec

- **Range:** 34:09–36:42 (~2:33)
- **Start cue (verbatim):** "Armani suits, Sulka Shirts was a shirt maker in Beverly Hills, and all sober colors, white shirts."
- **End cue (verbatim):** "Isn't that fantastic?"
- **Slug:** `andreessen-ovitz-crushed-hollywood-7am-meeting`

## What the agent should learn from this

1. The hook front-loads named figures and specific imagery (Jaguars, 7am).
2. The body uses a verbatim dialogue exchange as a center-of-post moment.
3. The closer is non-negotiable — verbatim brand + P.S. product + spaced-handle attribution.
4. The clip start cue (`Armani suits, Sulka Shirts...`) opens cold on rapid-fire specifics, not on transitional setup.
