# Example posts — David Baszucki on Founders (David Senra)

Source: [Roblox's David Baszucki Built the Biggest Playground on Earth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osuOwvEhVfQ) — Founders podcast, hosted by David Senra ( @davidsenra ).

These posts are the **canonical worked examples for the May 2026 narrative-style update**. Read them alongside the Andreessen examples — together they bracket the style: Andreessen for hook/violent-verb craft, Baszucki for narrative arc / sentence diversity / open engagement Qs.

## Style reference

These examples follow the post-May-2026 rules:

- **Narrative arc:** setup → inciting moment → consequence cascade → reflection → open Q
- **Sentence-length variation:** mix 4–6-word punches with 10–14-word narrative beats; parallel triplets used sparingly as deliberate beats
- **Open engagement Qs rooted in story tension** (not formulaic A/B binaries)
- **PAS-personalized P.S. para 1** + fixed playbook plug (Feynman/Munger/Musk + 90+ tools + 5,000+ founders/investors)
- **Verbatim clip cues** that match the audio exactly (including filler words at start/end of spoken phrase)

## Posts in this directory

- `A-sabbatical-vision.md` — Baszucki's 2-year detour applying for CEO jobs at companies he didn't found, and the vision-night that snapped him back. Tier 1 founder-psychology angle.
- `B-creator-1000.md` — Roblox's creator #1,000 grows faster than creator #1 — the platform's curve is flattening while every other platform sharpens. Tier 1 wealth-creation angle.

These two demonstrate the new style across two different kinds of stories — a personal arc (sabbatical) and a metric-led contrarian frame (creator curve).
