## Why this works

- **Hook (11 words):** specific shocking claim + named figure + named platform. Verb is `says`. No recency. The whole post is built to make this counterintuitive claim land.
- **Setup the gravity first, then the rupture.** The opening "Every major content platform of the last twenty years has obeyed the same gravity" sets up the universal pattern (winner-take-all sharpening) so Roblox going the other direction *means* something. Compare to "Marc Andreessen says AOL killed the early internet on a single day in September 1993" → "Before that day, the internet had maybe two million users" — same setup-the-world-before-the-rupture move.
- **Sentence-length rhythm.** "YouTube. TikTok. Instagram. Same curve. Sharper every year." — five short fragments build the gravitational claim before the reversal. "On every other platform, mature equals winner-take-all. On Roblox, mature means the floor keeps rising." — parallel longer sentences land the contrast cleanly.
- **Cascade beat.** "The creator at the bottom of the top thousand is growing faster than the king. So is creator #2,000 versus #2. So is creator #10,000 versus the rest." — three escalating beats let the reveal sink in, the way "two million → twenty million → five billion" works in the Andreessen post.
- **Engagement Q tied to story tension:** "Where would you actually start if you wanted to make a real living from your work?" — pulls the reader into Baszucki's reframe instead of asking them to pick a label. Anyone with ambition has a real answer to that.
- **No insight-stack pattern.** The post has 4 quotes but they're each tied into the narrative, not stacked. Quote → context → cascade → reflection.

## What the agent should learn from this

1. **Open by establishing the universal pattern, then announce the reversal.** "Every platform sharpens" → "Roblox is going the other direction." The contrarian claim is more powerful when the reader has just had the conventional wisdom restated.
2. **Three-beat parallel cascades land reveals.** "creator #1,000 versus #1. So is #2,000 versus #2. So is #10,000 versus the rest." — repetition with escalation makes the reveal sink in.
3. **The contrast sentence pair is a closing technique.** "On every other platform, mature equals winner-take-all. On Roblox, mature means the floor keeps rising." — twin sentences built around the same word do the philosophical work without preaching.
4. **The engagement Q can be longer if it earns it.** "Where would you actually start if you wanted to make a real living from your work?" is 15 words — but every word is loaded with the post's content. Open Qs can be longer than the old binary cap.
