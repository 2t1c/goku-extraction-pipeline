# No Recency Words

Source podcasts are typically months to years old by the time we extract from them. Recency words ("just revealed", "today", "now") make a post feel dishonest or stale once a reader checks the source.

## Banned words and phrases

In the hook, body, and any agent-voice framing:

- **just** + verb → ❌ "just revealed", "just said", "just announced", "just exposed"
- **recently** → ❌ "Andreessen recently said…"
- **today** (when implying current time) → ❌ "Andreessen says today that…"
- **now** (when implying current state) → ❌ "Elon now runs 120 reviews a day"
- **this week / this month / this year** → ❌ "this week's interview"
- **breaking** → ❌ "breaking: Andreessen says…"

## What to use instead

Plain reported-speech verbs:

- **says** ✓
- **explains** ✓
- **argues** ✓
- **told [host]** ✓ — "Andreessen told Senra that…"
- **calls** ✓ — "Andreessen calls it…"
- **describes** ✓ — "Andreessen describes Elon's method as…"
- **recounted** ✓ — past tense when relevant

For state of the world, use the speaker's own framing:

- ❌ "Elon now runs Tesla, SpaceX, and Starlink"
- ✓ "Andreessen says Elon runs Tesla, SpaceX, and Starlink" (the speaker's claim, in their tense)

For events, use past tense:

- ❌ "Sequoia's Mike Moritz is passing on Tesla"
- ✓ "Sequoia's Mike Moritz passed on Tesla in 2008"

## Verbatim quotes are exempt

Never alter a verbatim quote. If the speaker says "I just heard about this last week," the quote stays as-is. The ban applies only to your prose framing.

## Why this matters

Two reasons:

1. **Honesty.** When a reader clicks the source and finds the podcast is 18 months old, the post's "just revealed" framing reads as deceptive. They won't trust the next post.
2. **Evergreen reposts.** Posts written without recency words can be reposted indefinitely. Posts with "today" or "this week" become stale immediately and can't be recycled.

## Examples — fix the hook

| ❌ Bad | ✓ Good |
|---|---|
| Marc Andreessen just revealed how Harvard Business School was built on a broken theory | Marc Andreessen says Harvard Business School was built on a broken theory |
| Naval recently said the playbook is back | Naval said the playbook is back |
| Andreessen now believes managerialism is collapsing | Andreessen says managerialism is collapsing |
| Just announced: Elon runs 120 design reviews a day | Marc Andreessen says Elon runs 120 design reviews a day |

## When in doubt

Read the hook back as if the source podcast was 5 years old. If the framing still works, ship. If it sounds dated or false, rewrite.
