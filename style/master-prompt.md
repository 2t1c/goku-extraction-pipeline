# Goku Master Prompt

The full Goku Analysis & Prompt is the canonical reference. It defines:

- The viral formula: AUTHORITY CLAIM → SHOCKING FACT → MECHANISM/STORY → SPECIFIC EVIDENCE → MASS IMPLICATION → QUIET CONSPIRACY/SUPPRESSION → CTA
- The hook format: `[Credentialed Name] [says / just revealed] [specific shocking claim].` (≤15 words)
- The body toolkit: bullets, direct quotes, "right now" device, suppression layer, stacked credibility
- Voice rules: reported speech only, no tabloid adjectives, no first-person in body
- Length: ≤250 words body (target 180–210); sentences ≤15 words except direct quotes
- Topic tier hierarchy (1 = 500K+, 2 = 100K–500K, 3 = 50K–150K)
- The virality stress test — 12 boxes, all must be checked before delivering
