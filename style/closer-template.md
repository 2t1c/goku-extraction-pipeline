# Closer Template — current (May 2026)

The closer is the last 4 elements of every long-form Goku post for @GeniusGTX. **Brand CTA wording is verbatim. The P.S. is personalized per post but follows a fixed structure.**

## Order

After the body ends, in this exact order:

### 1. Engagement Question (write fresh, story-rooted, open)

**The engagement Q is rooted in the specific tension of the post you just wrote.** Not a formulaic A/B button-press. An open natural question the reader can actually answer with their own situation, opinion, or experience.

**The shape that works:**
- Drops the reader into the post's central tension as it applies to their own life
- Open-ended enough that anyone reading has a genuine answer
- Tied directly to the story you just told — uses its imagery or stakes
- One sentence, ≤16 words

**Good examples (from posts in the new narrative style):**

| Post topic | Question |
|---|---|
| AOL kills early internet | *"Was AOL right to open the gates?"* |
| Baszucki's 2-year sabbatical | *"How many people are walking past their own vision because the logical path looks safer?"* |
| Roblox creator #1,000 grows faster | *"Where would you actually start if you wanted to make a real living from your work?"* |
| Roblox runs as 9 companies | *"What would your team look like if every leader ran their group like a CEO on Monday?"* |
| Time-to-game = 0 | *"What's a piece of friction in your product so boring that nobody on your team is excited to fix it?"* |
| Roblox refuses to sell user data | *"What asset in your business are you about to monetize that you should refuse to part with for ten more years?"* |

**The deprecated style (do not use):**

❌ Formulaic A/B binaries: *"Capital or discipline?"* / *"Rent or own?"* / *"Org chart or operating system?"* — these are button-presses, not invitations to think. They make the post feel like a quiz, not a story.

❌ Generic gut-checks: *"What do you think?"* / *"Genius or villain?"* — disconnected from the specific story.

**Why open + story-rooted wins:** the post just walked the reader through a specific tension. The engagement Q invites them to apply that tension to their own world. Replies that result are substantive (and increasingly the algorithm rewards thoughtful comments over one-word reactions). The reader feels addressed, not surveyed.

**Avoid:**
- Pure binaries with no narrative tie ("X or Y?")
- Multi-clause stacked questions
- Questions about the post itself ("what was the most surprising part?")
- Generic "where in your life..." prompts that don't reference the post's actual story

### 2. Brand CTA (verbatim — never change)

```
If you're new here, @GeniusGTX is a gallery for the greatest minds in economics, psychology, and history. Follow along for more similar content.
```

### 3. P.S. — PAS personalization + fixed playbook plug

The P.S. has two paragraphs. The first is **personalized to the post** using PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution-bridge). The second + third are the **fixed playbook plug** — same wording every time, with the same authority association and social proof.

**Para 1 (personalized — write fresh):** open the wound the post just exposed. State the problem in one short sentence, agitate it with the cost or stakes in another. Tie it back to the post's central tension. Two sentences, ≤45 words total.

**Para 2 + 3 + closer line (fixed wording):**

```
I've made a free playbook on how to use and create your own mental models — the same thinking strategies Feynman, Munger, and Musk built their careers on. Plus a database of 90+ cognitive tools, trusted by 5,000+ founders and investors.

Grab your copy: https://besuperhuman.gumroad.com/l/mentalmodels
```

**Why this structure works:**
- The PAS para 1 makes the plug feel earned — it grows out of the post's argument instead of being tacked on
- "Feynman, Munger, and Musk" anchors the product to known authorities (every founder/investor reader respects all three)
- "90+ cognitive tools" is concrete; "trusted by 5,000+ founders and investors" is social proof targeted to the GeniusGTX audience
- "Grab your copy:" is the cleanest possible CTA — no "free" filler (the price is implied)

**Full P.S. example (Baszucki sabbatical post):**

```
P.S. The most expensive years of your career are usually the ones spent doing the logical thing instead of the right thing. The cost is invisible until intuition snaps you back — and most people never get that snap.

I've made a free playbook on how to use and create your own mental models — the same thinking strategies Feynman, Munger, and Musk built their careers on. Plus a database of 90+ cognitive tools, trusted by 5,000+ founders and investors.

```

### 4. Attribution (write fresh, with spaced @handles)

Format:

```
— [Expert Name] ( @handle ), [credential], on [Host Name]'s ( @host_handle ) [Show Name]
```

**Critical:** add single spaces inside parentheses around every @handle. Without spaces, X's auto-mention engine doesn't render the tags as clickable links.

✅ `— David Baszucki ( @DavidBaszucki ), founder & CEO of Roblox, on David Senra's ( @davidsenra ) Founders podcast`

❌ `— David Baszucki (@DavidBaszucki), founder & CEO of Roblox, on David Senra's (@davidsenra) Founders podcast`

## What's removed from the old format

- The old "bridge / reciprocity lens" intro ("One pattern worth keeping...", "One lens worth keeping...") is **deprecated**.
- The old P.S. wording ("I made a free toolkit breaking down 100+ mental models...") is **deprecated** — the new playbook line replaces it.
- Formulaic A/B binary engagement questions are **deprecated**. Use open story-rooted questions.
