# Word Count — target 240–250

Body length target is **240–250 words**. Hard ceiling: 250. Floor: 230.

## Why upper-end and not 180–210

The legacy Goku doc said 180–210. In practice, denser posts perform better:

- More named characters = more recognition triggers
- More specific evidence = more credibility hooks
- More verbatim quotes = more authority
- Posts at 210 words read as "a little short" and leave value on the table

Posts at 245 ride right up to the X character limit (~25,000 chars; long-form posts are typically 1,300–1,500 chars), which is where the algorithm rewards depth.

## How to count

**Count actual words. Don't estimate.** Models consistently undercount by 30–50 words. If your gut says "about 250," it's probably 200.

Use this rule: paste the body into a word counter, or count manually. Don't ship a post you haven't actually counted.

## What to do when a draft comes in short

If the body is at 200–225, **don't pad with filler.** Pad with substance — go back to the transcript and pull more from one of:

1. **Secondary named characters.** Did the speaker mention a related figure (a mentor, a competitor, a peer) you skipped? Add them.
2. **Parallels the speaker drew.** Did they compare the subject to another industry / era / person? Include the comparison.
3. **Brand or domain backstory.** Founding year, market position at the time, what the company looked like before the moment.
4. **The broader framing the speaker uses.** What's the bigger pattern they're describing? The headline anecdote is usually one example of a thesis — name the thesis.
5. **A second verbatim quote.** Most posts can comfortably fit 3–4 quotes. If you only have 2, find a third.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't repeat the hook** at the end of the body. Pad with new substance, not restated claims.
- **Don't add filler sentences** like "It's a story worth thinking about" or "This says a lot about today." Banned tabloid territory.
- **Don't pad quotes** by extending them past the verbatim cut. Quotes must remain exact.
- **Don't add a second "My Take" or hedge sentences** to bulk up word count. Keep the voice tight.

## What counts vs. what doesn't

**Counts toward the body word count:**
- Hook
- All body prose
- All bullets (count fragment words)
- All embedded quotes

**Does NOT count toward body:**
- Engagement Q (closer)
- Brand CTA (closer)
- P.S. Product CTA (closer)
- Attribution (closer)

The closer is fixed-template content, not body.

## Checking before delivery

The virality stress test in `master-prompt.md` includes the word count box. **Don't skip it.** Posts that come in at 195 and ship anyway are the ones that get returned with "this is too short."
